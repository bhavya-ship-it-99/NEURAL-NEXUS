## AI powered personalized weather and AQI Health advisory
<br>
AeroCare is a personalized environmental health platform that combines real-time AQI and weather data with a user’s age, medical history, and occupation to assess environmental health risks.
<br>
It provides location-based AQI/weather monitoring, personalized health advisories and precautions, environmental alerts with notifications, a 7-day weather forecast, and historical trends.
<br>
The project uses HTML/CSS/JavaScript for the frontend, FastAPI + SQLite for the backend, Open-Meteo for weather data, WAQI for AQI, and Groq for enhanced personalized recommendations.
